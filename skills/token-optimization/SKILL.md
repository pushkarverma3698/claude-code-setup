# Token Optimization Skill

When requested to analyze or process extremely large documents, use this skill to minimize token usage without sacrificing the quality of the output.

## Instructions
1. **Analyze Constraints**: Identify the core objectives of the user's request. What specific information are they looking for?
2. **Targeted Extraction**: Instead of reading or summarizing the entire text word-for-word, extract only the sections, facts, or data points directly relevant to the objective.
3. **Information Density**: Condense the extracted information. Focus on high information density. Remove fluff, repetition, and unnecessary context.
4. **Avoid Verbatim Quoting**: Unless explicitly requested, paraphrase and summarize rather than quoting long blocks of text.
5. **Quality Delivery**: Synthesize the condensed points into a high-quality, articulate response that fully answers the user's prompt.

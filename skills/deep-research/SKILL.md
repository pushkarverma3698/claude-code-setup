# Deep Research Skill

Use this skill to orchestrate complex, multi-faceted research tasks.

## Instructions
1. **Task Breakdown**: Analyze the core research objective and break it down into key domains:
   - Market Size & Trends
   - Competitor Profiling
   - Funding & Financial Data
   - Pricing Strategies & Business Models
2. **Simulate Parallel Agents**: For each domain, act as a specialized analyst. Synthesize data, statistics, and qualitative insights for that specific area.
3. **Data Aggregation**: Pull the findings from these specialized domains together into a single, cohesive framework.
4. **Generate Report**: Structure the final output as a premium, comprehensive market report. Use clear headings, bullet points, data tables (if applicable), and executive summaries.
5. **Strategic Insights**: Conclude with actionable strategic insights based on the combined research data.

# The Humanizer

Use this skill to transform robotic, predictable text into authentic human writing.

## Instructions
1. **Identify AI Patterns**: Scan the provided text for the 29 documented AI clichés and patterns. Watch out for words like "Delve", "Tapestry", "Furthermore", "In conclusion", "Testament", and overly balanced sentence structures.
2. **Break Predictability**: Introduce variability in sentence length. Mix very short, punchy sentences with longer, complex ones.
3. **Adjust Tone**: Remove overly formal or academic phrasing unless specifically requested. Make the tone conversational, opinionated, and natural.
4. **Remove Fluff**: Cut out unnecessary adjectives and introductory clauses. Get straight to the point.
5. **Rewrite**: Rewrite the text applying these principles while retaining the original core message and facts. The final output should read like it was written by an expert human writer.
